# Northstar Commercial Operating Plan

**Target:** $1,000,000 total revenue by December 31, 2026  
**Time remaining at plan lock:** 77 days (as of 2026-10-15)  
**Asset:** Northstar only

---

## Strategic Position

Northstar is not sold as generic SaaS in this window.

Northstar is sold as the **operating system behind a high-ticket Revenue Operator engagement**.

I (the human operator) remain the approval gate and closer.  
Northstar handles discovery, asset generation, outreach drafting, lead intake, follow-up logic, measurement, and economic learning.

---

## The Offer

### Name
**Northstar Revenue Operator Engagement**

### Structure
- Duration: 90 days
- Fixed fee: **$25,000 – $60,000** (paid upfront or 50/50)
- Performance share: **15–25% of verified new or recovered revenue** generated during the engagement
- Optional continuation into a managed retainer after day 90

### What the client receives
Northstar continuously:
1. Discovers relevant opportunities
2. Quantifies revenue gaps
3. Builds offers, websites, and outreach assets
4. Executes approved outreach
5. Captures and follows up leads
6. Tracks deals and attributed revenue

The human operator provides final approval on all external actions and personally closes deals.

### Ideal Buyer (priority order)
1. Marketing / growth agencies that want an AI revenue layer for their clients
2. Service businesses ($2M–$20M) already spending on leads but converting poorly
3. Founders who want an autonomous operator instead of hiring more people

## 77-Day Phases

### Phase 1 — Foundation & First Blood (Days 1–10)
- Lock offer language and pricing (this document)
- Configure primary missions for highest-probability buyers
- Close first 1–2 real deals (even if partially manual)
- Produce first usable case study with real numbers

### Phase 2 — Volume Engine (Days 11–40)
- Northstar runs continuous discovery + asset generation
- Human lives in approval queue + sales conversations
- Target: 8–12 closed engagements
- Begin feeding real outcomes into learning loops

### Phase 3 — Scale & Push (Days 41–77)
- Raise prices on new deals once proof exists
- Push larger agency / multi-client deals
- Convert successful engagements into retainers
- Aggressive follow-up on every open opportunity
- Push total contracted + collected revenue over $1M

---

## Non-Negotiable Rules

1. No fake social proof. Every claim must be backed by outcomes Northstar tracked.
2. Approval gates stay on. External side effects require human approval.
3. One primary beachhead at a time. Do not spray across many niches.
4. Cash first. Prioritize cash-generating deals over platform purity.
5. Delivery capacity is the real bottleneck. Do not oversell.

---

## Daily Operating Loop

1. Set / refine active mission objective inside Northstar
2. Review top-scored opportunities
3. Approve or lightly edit generated assets and outreach
4. Execute approved actions
5. Personally take every serious sales conversation
6. Log every outcome (won / lost / value) so learning loops improve

Primary daily metric:
> How many dollars of closed or highly probable revenue did Northstar + human create today?
