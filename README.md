# Northstar

**Autonomous AI Revenue Operator**

Northstar discovers opportunities, quantifies revenue gaps, builds assets, obtains approval, executes outreach, captures leads, follows up, and measures outcomes.

---

## Current Commercial Mission

**Target:** $1,000,000 total revenue by December 31, 2026

Northstar is operated as the engine behind high-ticket **Revenue Operator Engagements**.

## Revenue Loop

Discover opportunities → quantify revenue gaps → build assets → obtain approval → deploy or contact → capture leads → follow up → measure outcomes.

## Core Capabilities

- Public-business opportunity discovery
- Evidence-based revenue opportunity modeling
- AI-generated offers, websites, and outreach drafts
- Approval-gated Vercel publishing
- Approval-gated outbound email through Resend
- Website lead intake
- Persistent mission, opportunity, lead, asset, and revenue state
- Economic learning and decision loops
